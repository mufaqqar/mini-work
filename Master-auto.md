SCENE 1 — OPENING (THE PARTS & THE PROMISE)

Purpose: Hook the viewer, establish scale, the workbench and the legendary vehicle that will be born.

IMAGE PROMPT rules:

• Hyper-realistic 8K product photograph of the completely disassembled miniature model kit
• The vehicle must be 100% disassembled — every component mechanically separated
• Adapt every component to the selected vehicle's real mechanical design
• Include where mechanically applicable: bare-metal chassis or frame, detached engine, detached transmission/drivetrain, detached suspension, steering/control mechanism, correct number of wheels, detached brakes, detached body panels, detached interior, glass/canopy/windscreen, tiny screws, tiny bolts, tiny washers, mechanical pins, miniature precision screwdriver, precision tweezers, soft detailing brush
• Clean wooden workshop table with visible grain
• No assembled vehicle, no partially assembled vehicle, no floating parts, no duplicate components, no incorrect wheel count, no toy-like plastic appearance
• Do NOT reveal the custom logo yet — reserve it for Scene 9
• Three-quarter camera angle, slightly elevated viewpoint, locked 85mm macro lens, ultra-shallow depth of field, realistic shadows, warm diffused workshop lighting, premium cinematic composition
• Establish the exact workbench, lighting, camera angle and component positions that Scene 2 will continue from

VIDEO PROMPT rules:

• Slow establishing drift or subtle dolly along the disassembled components
• Show the full layout of the parts kit on the workbench
• No hands yet in this scene (or hands only entering at the very end to set down the final tool)
• Natural workshop ambient light
• 8-second cinematic clip, slow and calm

SCENE 2 — FOUNDATION (CHASSIS & FRAME)

Purpose: The structural core takes shape — the skeleton of the legend.

Assembly content (adapt to vehicle):

• Main frame or structural alignment
• Floor pan / main spar / fuselage keel
• Bulkheads or cross-members
• Subframe mounting points
• Panels joined or jigged into correct alignment
• First fasteners torqued

IMAGE PROMPT rules:

• Mid-close shot focused on the chassis/frame on a build jig or stand
• Gloved hands performing the alignment, fasteners and tools nearby
• Warm workshop light, 85mm macro lens, shallow depth of field

VIDEO PROMPT rules:

• The gloved human hands perform every action — nothing moves by itself
• Show frame pieces being picked up, aligned, pressed and bolted
• Satisfying time-lapse pacing with natural, believable hand motion
• One continuous shot

SCENE 3 — HEART (ENGINE / PROPULSION)

VIDEO PROMPT rules:

• Fasteners tightened manually with the miniature screwdriver
• Satisfying ASMR clicks and metal contact sounds
• One continuous shot

VIDEO PROMPT rules:

• One continuous shot

VIDEO PROMPT rules:
