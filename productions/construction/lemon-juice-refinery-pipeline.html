🍋 Lemon — Juice Refinery and Pipeline Installation

Miniature industrial operation inside a giant lemon · Construction Scene 02

Storyboard 02 Construction

🍽️ Food & Activity

Food: Giant Lemon

Activity: Juice Refinery and Pipeline Installation

Concept: A miniature refinery team has built a pumping station inside a halved lemon, extracting fresh juice through copper pipelines running across the yellow rind and down to collection vats.

🏗️ Workforce & Machinery

Workers (6): 2 pipeline technicians welding copper pipe sections, 1 pumping station operator monitoring pressure gauges, 1 crane operator hoisting a storage tank, 1 quality inspector sampling juice at a valve, 1 cargo loader filling glass bottles from a spigot.

Machinery: Miniature pumping station with brass pistons, mobile crane with jib, copper pipeline network, hand-operated bottle filler.

📖 Story & Challenge

A pipeline burst has flooded part of the refinery platform with sticky lemon juice. Workers scramble to divert the flow while the pumping station operator shuts down the main valve. The damaged pipe must be replaced before the rising juice reaches the electrical equipment.

💡 Lighting & Environment

Time: Midday bright sunlight

Lighting: High-contrast sunlight streaming through a window, creating sharp shadows. The bright yellow lemon skin reflects warm light. Industrial blue LED lamps illuminate the interior workspace.

Environment: White marble kitchen countertop. Blurred modern kitchen with stainless steel appliances in the background.

SCENE STRUCTURE: Top area — a brass collection funnel inserted into the exposed lemon flesh channels juice downward. Side area — copper pipelines with flanged joints run along the yellow rind, supported by miniature metal stands. Interior area — a cavity has been carved into the lemon segments where a pumping station with pressure gauges and valves sits. Base area — the cut lemon rests on a marble counter with juice pooling and dripping over the edge.

WORKFORCE: 6 workers total. 2 pipeline technicians in blue coveralls and welding masks join copper pipes with a miniature torch. 1 pumping station operator in a white hard hat monitors three brass pressure gauges and adjusts a wheel valve. 1 crane operator in a yellow vest sits in a red mini-mobile crane lifting a cylindrical steel storage tank. 1 quality inspector in a lab coat collects a juice sample in a glass vial. 1 cargo loader in brown apron fills dark green glass bottles from a brass spigot.

MACHINERY: A red mini-mobile crane with extendable jib lifting a steel tank. A brass pumping station with twin cylinders, copper pipes, pressure gauges, and a main wheel valve. A network of copper pipelines running along the lemon rind on metal stands. A bottle-filling station with brass spigot and wooden crate.

PHYSICAL PROPS: Copper pipes and elbows, brass fittings, pressure gauges, steel storage tank, glass bottles, wooden bottle crate, metal scaffolding, welding torch, glass vials, canvas tarps, warning tape, steel stands.

The rising juice level threatens to flood the lower walkway where the crane operator works. The team must isolate the damaged section and install a replacement before the entire refinery is flooded.
