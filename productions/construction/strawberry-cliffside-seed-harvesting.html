🍓 Strawberry — Cliffside Seed Harvesting

Miniature industrial operation on a giant strawberry · Construction Scene 05

Storyboard 05 Construction

🍽️ Food & Activity

Food: Giant Strawberry

Activity: Cliffside Seed Harvesting

Concept: Rope-access technicians descend the steep red cliff-like face of a giant strawberry, harvesting the tiny yellow seeds (achenes) from pockets along the surface while a platform crane supports them from the green calyx above.

🏗️ Workforce & Machinery

Workers (6): 2 rope-access technicians in full harness gear scraping seeds into collection bags, 1 crane operator managing a suspended platform, 1 cargo handler winching filled bags up to the rim, 1 botanist cataloging seed varieties, 1 safety officer monitoring from a lookout point on the calyx.

Machinery: Mobile crane on the calyx rim, motorized winch system, suspended aluminum work platform, rope descent systems with ascenders.

📖 Story & Challenge

A technician has spotted a rare golden seed — a mutation that could be the key to breeding new strawberry varieties. But it's located on an overhang where the red flesh is soft and slippery with juice. A dangerous rescue-style extraction is needed.

💡 Lighting & Environment

Time: Bright morning with clear skies

Lighting: Strong morning sunlight casting sharp shadows across the textured red surface. The glossy skin reflects the sky. Diffused fill light bounces from the white surface below.

Environment: White ceramic plate on a garden table. Blurred garden greenery and morning sky in the background.

🎬 Image Prompt

Macro tilt-shift photography of a giant strawberry being harvested for its seeds by tiny realistic miniature human workers on its cliff-like surface.

FOOD APPEARANCE: A colossal strawberry shaped like a steep red mountain. The skin is deep ruby red with a glossy sheen, transitioning to lighter pink near the shoulders. Hundreds of tiny yellow achenes (seeds) are embedded in shallow dimples across the entire surface in a spiral pattern. The skin shows natural variations — darker red in the sun, lighter pink in the shade. The green calyx at the top is a star-shaped crown of fresh green sepals with visible veins and tiny hairs. Moisture beads and dew drops cling to the surface. Tiny scratches and natural blemishes add realism.

SCENE STRUCTURE: Top area — the green calyx crown has been turned into a operations base with wooden platforms wedged between the sepals, a mobile crane anchored to the stem, and winch equipment. Side area — the steep vertical face of the strawberry is a cliff-like drop where two rope technicians abseil down; they have a suspended platform partway down. Interior area — not applicable (surface operation). Base area — the strawberry rests on a white ceramic plate with a few loose seeds and a tiny pool of pink juice spreading from the contact point.

WORKFORCE: 6 workers total. 2 rope-access technicians in red and blue full-body harnesses, helmets, and ascender devices abseil down the vertical strawberry face — one scrapes achenes into a canvas collection bag, the other documents a golden seed with a tiny camera. 1 crane operator in a yellow vest sits on the calyx operating a red mini-mobile crane with a boom supporting their platform. 1 cargo handler in brown workwear operates a hand winch, pulling up a bulging canvas sack of seeds. 1 botanist in a white lab coat examines a seed sample under a magnifying glass on a small table. 1 safety officer in an orange vest stands at the edge of the calyx with a pair of binoculars.

MACHINERY: A red mini-mobile crane with a telescoping boom mounted on the calyx platform. A motorized winch drum with steel cable running through pulley blocks mounted on the stem. An aluminum suspended work platform with safety railings hanging halfway down the cliff face. Rope descent systems with mechanical ascenders, descenders, and carabiners.

PHYSICAL PROPS: Canvas seed collection bags, aluminum work platform, steel cables, rope coils, ascender devices, safety harnesses, hard hats, binoculars, magnifying glass, collection vials, wooden platforms, hand tools, carabiners, warning flags, clipboard.

STORY AND CHALLENGE: A rare golden achene has been discovered on a precarious overhang where the strawberry flesh is soft and wet. The technician leans out from the suspended platform, secured by a safety line, reaching for the golden seed while the crane operator holds the platform steady. Below him, the red cliff face drops away to the white plate far below. The seed glitters in the morning light — a scientific prize worth the risk. The safety officer watches through binoculars, ready to signal if conditions become too dangerous.

LIGHTING: Bright morning sunlight (approx 5000K) from a clear sky, creating strong directional light with sharp shadows. The glossy red skin reflects the blue sky as subtle highlights. Warm fill light bounces from the white ceramic plate below. The green calyx is backlit, creating a bright green rim light effect. Dew drops sparkle with specular highlights.

ENVIRONMENT: White ceramic plate with a subtle rim and slight shadow beneath the strawberry. A blurred garden table setting in the background with potted plants, soft green foliage bokeh, and a bright morning sky with thin clouds.

CAMERA: 100mm macro lens, tilt-shift miniature effect, shallow depth of field focused on the technician reaching for the golden seed. Cinematic 9:16 vertical composition. Extreme detail on the strawberry skin texture and the dew droplets. Natural color grading with saturated reds and fresh greens. No text, logo, or watermark.
