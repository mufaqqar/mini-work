🥑 Avocado — Pit Drilling and Research Facility

Miniature industrial operation inside a giant avocado · Construction Scene 06

Storyboard 06 Construction

🍽️ Food & Activity

Food: Giant Avocado

Activity: Pit Drilling and Research Facility

Concept: A research expedition has set up a drilling facility on the surface of a giant avocado pit, boring core samples from the massive brown seed while scientists study the mysterious compounds found deep within.

🏗️ Workforce & Machinery

Workers (6): 2 drilling engineers operating a core drill rig on the pit surface, 1 sample technician collecting core samples into glass tubes, 1 research scientist analyzing samples under a microscope, 1 crane operator moving equipment, 1 safety inspector checking the drill rig stability on the curved pit surface.

Machinery: Miniature diamond-core drill rig with mast and hydraulic feed, mobile crane with flatbed, portable laboratory module with microscope, sample storage rack.

📖 Story & Challenge

The drill has struck a pocket of viscous golden oil inside the avocado pit — a previously unknown substance. The research team must carefully extract a sample while the unstable borehole threatens to collapse around the drill string.

💡 Lighting & Environment

Time: Late afternoon laboratory light

Lighting: Cool overhead fluorescent laboratory light mixing with warm task lamps. The creamy green avocado flesh glows with a soft chartreuse translucency. The brown pit has warm earthy tones under the light.

Environment: Polished granite laboratory countertop. Blurred laboratory background with glass beakers and scientific equipment.
