🍍 Pineapple — Vertical Railway Construction

Miniature industrial operation on a giant pineapple · Construction Scene 07

Storyboard 07 Construction

🍽️ Food & Activity

Food: Giant Pineapple

Activity: Vertical Railway Construction

Concept: Railway engineers build a spiraling cog railway that climbs the diamond-patterned surface of a giant pineapple, navigating the hexagonal facets to transport cargo from the base to the crown.

🏗️ Workforce & Machinery

Workers (7): 2 railway workers laying steel tracks on wooden sleepers, 1 locomotive engineer driving a tiny steam locomotive, 1 crane operator lifting rail sections via a boom, 1 surveyor mapping the spiral route, 1 blasting specialist preparing to clear a rock-like pineapple eye, 1 foreman coordinating from a switchback platform.

Machinery: Miniature steam locomotive with three freight cars, mobile crane with jib, cog railway track sections, portable winch, surveying transit.

📖 Story & Challenge

The spiral railway must cross a massive pineapple "eye" — a tough diamond-shaped facet that protrudes like a cliff. The blasting specialist must carefully crack it while the railway workers brace the track behind them. The steam locomotive waits, loaded with supplies, for the path ahead to open.

💡 Lighting & Environment

Time: Tropical golden hour

Lighting: Warm golden sunset light from a tropical sky, creating long shadows across the pineapple's geometric surface. The golden-yellow fruit glows with warm saturated tones. Deep shadows in the crevices between facets.

Environment: Rough wooden market table. Blurred tropical market background with hanging bananas and woven baskets in soft focus.

MACHINERY: A miniature coal-fired steam locomotive with brass bell, smokestack, cowcatcher, and three wooden freight cars with steel wheels. A mobile crane with a lattice jib and steel cables. If the blast is too strong, it could damage the track below. The locomotive hisses steam impatiently as the crew works against the fading tropical daylight.
