🐉 Dragon Fruit — Crystal Extraction Facility

Miniature industrial operation inside a giant dragon fruit · Construction Scene 11

Storyboard 11 Construction

🍽️ Food & Activity

Food: Giant Dragon Fruit (Pitaya)

Activity: Crystal Extraction Facility

Concept: A miniature mining operation drills into the vibrant magenta flesh of a split dragon fruit, extracting shimmering natural crystal formations embedded near the skin while avoiding the thousands of tiny black seeds.

🏗️ Workforce & Machinery

Workers (7): 3 crystal miners with miniature jackhammers and chisels extracting crystal formations, 1 crane operator on a mobile crane hoisting extracted crystal clusters, 1 transport operator driving a flatbed trolley, 1 safety inspector monitoring seed-fall zones with a radio, 1 foreman with a clipboard directing operations.

Machinery: Miniature mobile crane with jib, flatbed cargo trolley, conveyor belt system, industrial LED floodlights.

📖 Story & Challenge

The main crystal vein runs dangerously close to the outer skin. If the miners drill too deep, they will pierce the skin and cause a structural collapse. A recent tremor (from a falling seed) has dislodged a massive seed that now blocks the main transport tunnel. The foreman must decide whether to blast through the seed (risking the crystal vein) or drill a costly bypass tunnel.

💡 Lighting & Environment

Time: Late afternoon

Lighting: Warm golden sunlight from a west-facing window creating dramatic contrast against the magenta dragon fruit flesh. Vibrant magenta/pink light bounces off every surface from the fruit interior. Cool white industrial LED floodlights illuminate the mining face.

Environment: Dark wooden cutting board on a kitchen counter. Blurred background with ceramic fruit bowl and soft window light.

FOOD APPEARANCE: A colossal dragon fruit split open to reveal its vibrant magenta interior. The flesh is densely speckled with thousands of tiny black seeds scattered throughout like peppercorns. The outer skin is a dramatic magenta-pink with green-tipped overlapping scales (bracts) that curl outward at the edges. A thick band of pale white pith separates the skin from the magenta flesh. Shimmering translucent crystal-like formations — pale pink and white — protrude from the flesh near the skin boundary, catching the light with internal refractions. Some crystals are clustered in veins running parallel to the outer edge. The exposed cut surface glistens with moisture.

STORY AND CHALLENGE: The main crystal vein runs dangerously close to the outer pink skin of the dragon fruit. A large black seed — the size of a boulder relative to the workers — has dislodged from the upper flesh during a minor tremor and now blocks the transport tunnel that connects the mining pit to the trolley loading area. The foreman and lead miner argue near the seed: one hand points a route for drilling a bypass tunnel around the seed (costly and time-consuming), while the other gestures toward jackhammering the seed directly (risking vibrations that could crack the fragile crystal vein and the outer skin). The safety inspector watches from a safe distance, one hand raised in a warning gesture.
