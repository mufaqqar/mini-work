🍫 Chocolate — Deep-Crack Mining Colony

Miniature industrial operation inside a giant chocolate bar · Construction Scene 08

Storyboard 08 Construction

🍽️ Food & Activity

Food: Giant Dark Chocolate Bar

Activity: Deep-Crack Mining Colony

Concept: A mining colony has been established inside a deep crack in a giant dark chocolate bar, where miners extract cacao crystals and rare chocolate gems from the fractured depths using industrial drilling rigs and rope-access systems.

🏗️ Workforce & Machinery

Workers (8): 2 deep-crack miners with pneumatic drills extracting chocolate chunks, 2 rope technicians lowering equipment into the crack, 1 conveyor operator feeding a sorting belt, 1 foreman supervising from the crack edge, 1 cargo loader packing chocolate specimens into crates, 1 geologist examining a crystal formation.

Machinery: Pneumatic drilling rig on a tripod, motorized conveyor belt system, winch and pulley rig, mobile lighting tower, portable rock crusher.

📖 Story & Challenge

A massive chocolate crystal has been discovered deep in the crack — but it's wedged in an unstable fissure that could snap shut if the wrong support is removed. The miners must carefully extract the crystal while installing emergency shoring to prevent the crack from collapsing.

💡 Lighting & Environment

Time: Cool interior lighting

Lighting: Dramatic top-down spotlights creating deep shadows in the crack. The dark chocolate absorbs most light, so warm industrial work lamps (3000K) create pools of illumination. The chocolate surface has subtle specular highlights from its glossy sheen.

Environment: Polished marble pastry slab in a patisserie kitchen. Blurred kitchen background with mixing bowls and cooling racks.

The fracture surface is rough and irregular with sharp edges and deep fissures. The chocolate has a subtle oil sheen. A few thin cracks radiate from the main fracture, creating a network of smaller crevices. The underside shows the molded pattern of the original bar.

SCENE STRUCTURE: Top area — the smooth top surface of the chocolate bar has been set up as a staging platform with the conveyor system, tool storage, and a winch rig. Side area — the vertical broken edge shows the fracture profile, with rope lines hanging down into the crack. Interior area — the deep crack between the two halves forms a V-shaped chasm where miners work; the walls are jagged dark chocolate with visible crystal veins. Base area — the chocolate bar rests on a polished marble slab with tiny chocolate fragments scattered around and a subtle cocoa powder dusting.

WORKFORCE: 8 workers total. 2 deep-crack miners in orange reflective vests and headlamps operate a pneumatic drill mounted on a tripod in the crack, extracting chocolate chunks from the fracture wall. 2 rope technicians in red harnesses and helmets use a winch system to lower a steel cage with tools into the crack. 1 conveyor operator in blue coveralls monitors a belt system carrying chocolate fragments up from the crack to a sorting area. 1 foreman in a yellow hard hat stands at the crack edge with a radio and clipboard. 1 cargo loader in brown workwear packs large chocolate crystals into wooden crates lined with straw. 1 geologist in a white coat uses a hand lens to examine a glittering chocolate crystal formation on the crack wall.

MACHINERY: A pneumatic drilling rig with a yellow tripod, air hose, and rotating drill bit designed for chocolate. A motorized conveyor belt with steel rollers and side walls climbing from the crack interior. A winch and pulley system with steel cable, pulley blocks, and a lifting cage. A mobile lighting tower with four LED floodlamps. A portable rock crusher with a hopper for breaking large chocolate chunks.

PHYSICAL PROPS: Wooden crates with straw padding, steel lifting cage, canvas tool bags, pneumatic air hoses, headlamps, hand lenses, specimen vials, safety harnesses, steel shoring beams, wooden wedges, cocoa-dusted canvas tarps, warning lamps, tool chest.

STORY AND CHALLENGE: In the deepest part of the crack, the geologist has discovered a fist-sized chocolate crystal — a rare formation of pure cocoa butter crystals that glitter like a gemstone. But it's embedded in a section of the fracture wall that is under immense pressure. Cracks are spreading from the extraction point. The miners must install hydraulic jacks to support the wall while carefully chiseling around the crystal. One wrong move and the entire fissure could collapse, burying the crystal and the workers below.

LIGHTING: Dramatic top-down spotlights (approx 4000K) create deep, moody shadows inside the crack. Warm industrial work lamps (3000K) mounted on tripods illuminate specific work areas, casting warm orange pools of light on the dark chocolate. The crack's deeper sections are shrouded in near-darkness, with only miner headlamps providing pools of white light. The chocolate crystal glitters with specular highlights when hit by light. The glossy top surface reflects the work lamps.

ENVIRONMENT: Polished white marble pastry slab with subtle veining, scattered with tiny dark chocolate fragments and a dusting of cocoa powder. A blurred patisserie kitchen background with stainless steel mixing bowls, cooling racks, and hanging utensils in soft bokeh.

CAMERA: 100mm macro lens, tilt-shift miniature effect, shallow depth of field focused on the crystal extraction point. Cinematic 9:16 vertical composition emphasizing the depth of the crack. Extreme detail on the chocolate's crystalline fracture surface and glossy top. Dark moody color grading with warm accent lights. No text, logo, or watermark.
