🍫 The Tiny Builders Who Built a Giant Chocolate Factory

Miniature industrial operation · Construction Scene

Construction Storyboard Construction

🍫 Food & Activity

Food: Giant Milk Chocolate Bar

Activity: Chocolate Factory Construction

Concept: Tiny builders construct a working miniature chocolate factory on top of and inside a giant milk chocolate bar, building conveyor systems, tempering vats, molding stations, and packaging lines that process real chocolate from raw cacao chunks into finished wrapped bars.

🏗️ Workforce & Machinery

Workers (10): 2 structural engineers welding steel beams for the factory framework, 3 assembly workers installing conveyor belts and machinery, 2 plumbers connecting chocolate pipelines between vats, 1 foreman directing from a raised platform with a blueprint, 1 electrician wiring the control panel, 1 quality inspector examining a freshly molded chocolate bar.

Machinery: Miniature tower crane, motorized conveyor belt system, copper tempering vats, chocolate molding press, wrapping machine, steam pipes, control panel with tiny indicator lights.

📖 Story & Challenge

The factory's main chocolate pipeline has a critical leak — liquid chocolate is spraying from a cracked copper joint, coating the workers and machinery below. The plumbers must replace the damaged section while the crane holds the heavy vat steady, and the pipeline must be reconnected before the chocolate cools and solidifies inside, which would permanently block the entire system.

💡 Lighting & Environment

Time: Warm interior lighting

Lighting: Warm golden light (3200K) from overhead industrial lamps illuminating the factory floor. The chocolate surfaces absorb light creating deep rich shadows. Copper vats and steel beams catch warm specular highlights. Steam from the tempering vats creates soft diffusion. The chocolate has a glossy sheen where warm and a matte texture where cool.

Environment: Polished white marble pastry slab in a professional chocolate kitchen. Blurred background with stainless steel equipment, hanging copper molds, and warm ambient kitchen light.
