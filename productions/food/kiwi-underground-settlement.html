🥝 Kiwi — Underground Settlement

Miniature residential settlement built inside a giant kiwi · Food Scene

Food Storyboard 03 Cinematic Food

🍽️ Food & Activity

Food: Giant Ripe Kiwi Fruit

Activity: Underground Settlement Construction

Concept: Tiny settlers carve a network of connected tunnels and chamber rooms into the vibrant green kiwi flesh, building a miniature underground village with wooden huts, rope bridges, and communal spaces suspended between the central pale core and the fuzzy brown skin.

🏗️ Workforce & Machinery

Workers (7): 2 tunnel excavators with miniature pickaxes and shovels carving passageways into the green flesh, 1 structural engineer installing timber support beams along a tunnel ceiling, 2 builders constructing a wooden hut in a cleared chamber, 1 rope-bridge rigger lashing hemp ropes between adjacent flesh pillars, 1 supply runner carrying bundles of timber planks through the tunnel network.

Machinery: Miniature tracked excavator with a front bucket, hand-operated wooden crane for lifting beams, pulley rope system for hauling supplies.

📖 Story & Challenge

The settlers have reached a delicate zone near the outer skin where the flesh is softer and mixed with tiny black seeds that crumble unpredictably. A tunnel section has partially collapsed, trapping a supply of timber planks behind a wall of loose kiwi flesh. The structural engineer must design a support system to safely dig through the collapsed area while the excavator operator works carefully to avoid triggering another cave-in. Meanwhile, a scout reports a vast untouched chamber on the opposite side of the core that could house the settlement's central hall.

💡 Lighting & Environment

Time: Midday with artificial lantern light

Lighting: Soft diffused light filtering through the kiwi skin creates a natural green glow throughout the flesh tunnels. Warm orange lantern light from hanging oil lamps provides task illumination in the carved chambers. Dramatic contrast between cool green ambient light and warm artificial sources.

Environment: The kiwi rests on a ceramic plate on a kitchen counter. Softly blurred background with a fruit bowl and a window casting natural daylight. The fuzzy brown skin of the kiwi creates a textured exterior wall.
