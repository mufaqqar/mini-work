🥭 Mango — Golden Flesh Quarry

Miniature industrial operation inside a giant mango · Food Scene

Food Storyboard 01 Cinematic Food

🍽️ Food & Activity

Food: Giant Ripe Mango

Activity: Golden Flesh Quarry

Concept: Tiny quarry workers carve precise golden blocks from the dense mango flesh near the central seed, transporting them via conveyor and crane to waiting cargo crates.

🏗️ Workforce & Machinery

Workers (6): 2 quarry cutters with miniature pickaxes and steel chisels extracting golden mango blocks, 1 crane operator on a mini-crawler crane hoisting cut blocks, 2 cargo handlers loading blocks onto a conveyor belt, 1 surveyor measuring the next cutting zone with a wooden measuring rod.

Machinery: Miniature crawler crane, motorized conveyor belt system, four-wheeled cargo trolley.

📖 Story & Challenge

The quarry face is approaching the enormous central seed — a single misplaced strike could fracture the entire mango half. The surveyor has discovered that the flesh closest to the seed is denser and glows with a deeper golden hue, making it far more valuable. Workers must carefully extract these premium blocks without cracking the seed or collapsing the quarry walls.

💡 Lighting & Environment

Time: Early morning

Lighting: Warm golden sunlight (approx 3500K) streaming from a kitchen window, illuminating the cut mango surface. The mango flesh glows like amber with translucent backlighting. Deep warm shadows on the far side of the quarry. Soft rim light on workers from the golden translucency of the flesh.

Environment: Rustic wooden cutting board on a weathered kitchen counter. Softly blurred background with ceramic mixing bowls and a sunlit window. Fine flour dust particles visible in the morning light beam.
